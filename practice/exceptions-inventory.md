# Exercise: the exceptions inventory

*A 30-minute team exercise for [03 Knowledge](../principles/03-knowledge.md). Run it on Monday.*

Every system has rules that only someone remembers. This exercise finds them before an
AI agent trips over them.

## How to run it
1. **Pick one system** your team owns or depends on, ideally one AI will soon read.
2. **Invite the people who get asked.** Support, long-tenured colleagues, the "ask Maria" person.
3. **Ask three questions** and write every answer down:
   - What do people ask you to do *outside* the normal process?
   - Which field or value doesn't mean what its name says?
   - What does every new colleague get told in their first month that isn't documented?
4. **Score each exception:** how often does it happen, and what breaks if an AI ignores it?
5. **Fix the top three:** turn them into a field, a value, a documented rule or a governance decision.
