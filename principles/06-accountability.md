# 06 · Accountability

> **Automation does not automate responsibility.**  
> Responsibility does not disappear when human intervention does.

*Group: about how we act on it*

## What it means
AI can remove the approval step. It cannot remove the responsibility. For every decision
an AI system takes or supports, a person owns it, and everyone knows who.

## Why it matters for AI
When a person typed a value by hand, it was clear who did it. When an agent does it a
thousand times a minute, it's easy for everyone to assume someone else is responsible.
That is when mistakes stay in production.

## The story
[Zero annulments](../stories/zero-annulments.md): somebody in IT support typed ANNULLED.
Who owned that decision?

## In practice
- Name a business owner for every AI workflow that changes data or triggers action.
- Define what the agent may do alone, what needs a human, and what it must never do.
- Build a way to stop, roll back and correct, and test it before launch.
- Keep humans as the custodians: AI makes answers faster; trust makes decisions faster.

## Questions to ask
- If this goes wrong, whose phone rings?
- What can the agent do without asking, and who decided that?
- How do we undo it?
