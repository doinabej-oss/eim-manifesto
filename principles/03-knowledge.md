# 03 · Knowledge

> **Make what the organisation knows, knowable.**  
> AI cannot leverage knowledge the enterprise never made explicit.

*Group: about the information*

## What it means
Systems hold the data. People hold the context. Every organisation runs on exceptions,
workarounds and "everyone knows that" rules that exist only in someone's memory. They
work because a person is in the loop and can be asked.

## Why it matters for AI
AI cannot whisper. It cannot lean over to a colleague and ask what a field *really* means,
or why a record was handled differently last year, unless we have built that path for it.
An agent can only consult the people, rules and known exceptions we connect it to. If a
rule isn't written down somewhere the system can reach, the agent can't apply it, and it
may not even know that something is missing.

Context (01) is meaning held by *structure*. Knowledge is meaning held by *people*.

## The story
[Zero annulments](../stories/zero-annulments.md): the query was correct. The rule was in
someone's memory.

## In practice
- Run an [exceptions inventory](../practice/exceptions-inventory.md): list the rules only someone remembers.
- Turn the top exceptions into fields, values or documented rules, not chat messages.
- When a long-tenured colleague leaves, capture the *why*, not just the *where*.
- Keep the knowledge base next to the data, versioned and owned.

## Questions to ask
- Who would you ask if this answer looked strange? Is what they know written anywhere?
- Which "unofficial" values or workarounds exist in this system?
- What does the AI not know that every senior colleague does?
