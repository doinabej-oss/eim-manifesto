# 04 · Governance

> **Decide who defines what things mean.**

*Group: about how we act on it*

## What it means
Who decides what ACTIVE means? Which source is the truth for a customer, a contract, a
price? Governance is agreeing on definitions, sources and rules, and on who may change them.

Governance is the banks, AI is the river. Banks don't stop the river. They let it
flow fast without flooding the town.

## Why it matters for AI
Organisations are adopting AI faster than they govern it. IDC research for Lenovo (2026)
found 60% of organisations in late-stage AI adoption, but only 27% with a comprehensive
AI governance framework. Agents act on definitions; if nobody owns the definitions,
nobody owns what the agents do.

## The story
[Zero annulments](../stories/zero-annulments.md): the STATUS field said ACTIVE. The TITLE
said ANNULLED. Who decided which one counts?

## In practice
- Name an owner for each critical definition (status values, lifecycle states, master data).
- Pick one system of record per entity, and write it down.
- Make "who may change this value, and how" explicit for fields AI acts on.
- Review new AI use cases against the definitions they depend on before launch.

## Questions to ask
- Who decides what this value means? Can I find their name in under a minute?
- If two systems disagree, which one wins?
- Was this definition agreed, or did it just happen?
