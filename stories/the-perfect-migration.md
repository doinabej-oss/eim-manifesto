# The perfect migration

*Illustrates: [01 Context](../principles/01-context.md) · [02 Quality × Quantity](../principles/02-quality-x-quantity.md)*

A story from the world of investigations. By every measure, the migration was a success.
One and a half million files of digital evidence moved to the new repository. The numbers
matched: 100% migrated, zero files lost.

But there was a catch.

In the old repository, nobody needed to write down where a piece of evidence came from.
The **folder path** said it. A file sitting under a case and a source *was* that case's
evidence, from that source. The structure held the provenance. In the new repository,
everything landed in one flat structure.

The migration moved the files. It didn't move the meaning of where they had been.

**We migrated every tree. And we lost the forest.**

## What we took from it
- A migration can be technically perfect and still lose information.
- Structure is information. Before you flatten it, write down what it meant.
- An AI reading those files today would understand every sentence, and still not know what
  each document *is*.
